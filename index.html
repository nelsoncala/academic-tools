Open academic tools

Tools for quantitative genetics, biostatistics and animal science education.

Educational web applications, scripts and resources for teaching and research.

This portfolio collects academic tools developed by Nelson Cala-Moreno, PhD, to support teaching, reproducible analysis and applied learning in quantitative genetics, animal breeding, biostatistics, experimental design and animal science.

Academic purpose

These tools are designed as teaching and research support resources, especially for veterinary medicine, animal science, genetics and biostatistics courses.
